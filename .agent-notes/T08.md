## Observation: `regex` timeout needs a genuinely exponential pattern; ASCII flag breaks \p{L}
- **Context**: Building the T08 interpreter timeout and dialect handling.
- **Finding**: `regex.fullmatch(..., timeout=)` raises `TimeoutError`, but the module optimizes
  `(a+)+$` and `(a*)*b` (return instantly); `^(a|aa)+$` on "a"*60+"!" does time out.
  `regex.ASCII` makes `\p{L}` fail on non-ASCII letters, so it cannot mimic Java's ASCII `\w`.
- **Impact**: Use `^(a|aa)+$` for timeout fixtures; Unicode `\w`/`\s`/case folding stay broader than Java's.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: upstream skips comments in preprocessing, blank lines via CommandNope
- **Context**: Locating where the command loop skips comments/blank lines.
- **Finding**: `'` and `/' '/` lines are dropped by `preproc2/ReadFilterQuoteComment.java:45-80`;
  blank lines are matched by `CommandNope` (added by `CommonCommands.java:63`). The loser-diagram
  error chosen by `PSystemErrorUtils.merge` is max `score()` = `trace.size()*10+...` (first wins ties).
- **Impact**: Models must include CommandNope for blank lines; the interpreter also skips blank lines
  when nothing matched.
- **Confidence**: High
