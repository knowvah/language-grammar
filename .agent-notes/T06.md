## Observation: all Pattern2 regexes are case-insensitive; model regex parts carry no flag
- **Context**: translating IRegex trees (Pattern2.java:114 `Pattern.CASE_INSENSITIVE`).
- **Finding**: literals get `ci: true`, but `{regex}` parts have no ci marker; interpreters/emitters must apply IGNORECASE to regex parts of IRegex-engine rules. Opaque entries from plain `Pattern.compile` (Colors, Ditaa, Dot) are case-sensitive.
- **Impact**: T08/T09/T10/T11 must treat regex parts as ci for provenance.engine IRegex.
- **Confidence**: High
