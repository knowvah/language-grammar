- **Confidence**: High (oracle and legacy-only probe)

## Observation: `java -jar -syntax` shows the first factory that accepts, not the factory you are probing
- **Context**: probing ActivityDiagramFactory with blocks like `--> A`.
- **Finding**: SequenceDiagramFactory runs before the legacy activity factory and accepts `--> A` and `note right: n` / `--> B`, so whole-jar probes say nothing about the legacy factory. A scratch probe that empties `PSystemBuilder.singleton.factories` except the named factories (reflection on the private `factories` list, PSystemBuilder.java:128-130) gives per-factory verdicts and messages.
- **Impact**: per-factory semantics need a factory-restricted probe; family fixtures still need syntax that every factory rejects.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: legacy activity state upstream checks at execute time
- **Context**: modelling ActivityDiagramFactory.
- **Finding**: (1) `lastEntityConsulted` starts null: a source-less link, long link or `if` fails ("No such activity", "No such entity", "No if possible at this point"). An activity note creates the start node instead (command/note/CommandFactoryNoteActivity.java:197-199). A note on a link fails without a link ("No link defined"). (2) The if and group stacks are independent; unclosed ifs and partitions are accepted. (3) The first `}` at the root is accepted and leaves an orphan state, as in class. Links, ifs, partitions, activity notes and `}` then fail, while common commands, hide/show, notes on links and `else`/`endif` still work. (4) Unknown colors in entity, partition and note color slots fail ("No such color"); `-[#zzzz]->` link styles do not.
- **Impact**: the model has 9 state contexts. The leniencies and unknown colors are gap fixtures.
- **Confidence**: High (legacy-only probes)

## Observation: interpreter-only `set` in head entries does not cost Lark much
- **Context**: plantuml-ts Lark sample timeouts after adding the state contexts.
- **Finding**: the CPU time for class/pudixe-16-deki169 (130 lines) was 19 s with the baseline grammar, 27 s with activity-legacy, and 30 s with the head `set` removed. Small blocks were 2-7 s in all three. Wall time under concurrent load reached 85 s, so parity timeouts at 10 s are noisy.
- **Impact**: measure Lark cost with `time.process_time()` and interleaved grammars, not with wall-clock parity timeouts.
- **Confidence**: Medium (two rounds, machine shared with other agents)
