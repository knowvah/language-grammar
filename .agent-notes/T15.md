## Observation: Creole never changes syntax-check acceptance
- **Context**: Building model/creole.yaml and its oracle fixtures.
- **Finding**: Labels are parsed by `klimt/creole/legacy/CreoleParser.java:142-185` when the diagram is drawn, not when commands are matched. The stock jar accepts all 9 creole fixtures, including malformed ones (unclosed `**`, unknown color/sprite/icon/emoji, missing image, `<size:99999999999>`, `<u:#abc>`, unterminated `<code>`). Out-of-range numbers throw NumberFormatException only at render time (`klimt/creole/command/CommandCreoleSizeChange.java:86`).
- **Impact**: Creole cannot supply neg fixtures. Family tasks should not expect label markup to change a verdict.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: Creole case sensitivity is split between starter and body
- **Context**: Choosing `ci` for creole tags.
- **Finding**: The command lookup key is the first two chars, case-sensitive (`klimt/creole/legacy/StripeSimple.java:297-306`). Bodies are case-insensitive: Pattern2, or UBrex, which lowercases both sides. So `<cOLOR:red>` works, but `<COLOR:red>`, `<W>`, `<SUP>`, `<SIZE:..>` render literally. Only b/i/p/u/s/d/back register uppercase starters (`klimt/font/FontStyle.java:48-70`).
- **Impact**: Tag names are modelled as `c(?i:olor)` with `ci: false`.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: named colors live in ColorTrieNode, hex accepts 1/3/6/8 digits
- **Context**: Extracting upstream's color table.
- **Finding**: The 154 names are registered at `klimt/color/ColorTrieNode.java:55-209`; `HColorSet.names()` only delegates. Hex parsing accepts 1, 3, 6 or 8 digits, not 4 (`klimt/color/HColorSet.java:122-157`). `transparent`, `background` and `automatic` are special-cased (`HColorSet.java:82-86`) and are not in the table.
- **Impact**: model/shared.yaml COLOR_NAME mirrors the table; COLOR_SPECIAL holds the three specials.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: random corpus samples miss rare fragment variants
- **Context**: Mutation-testing the 50-line equivalence check.
- **Finding**: A plain random sample of lines matching ARROW_STYLE never contained `[dotted]`, so dropping `dotted` from the structured form went undetected. Sampling one line per distinct set of matched fragments first catches it.
- **Impact**: tests/layers/test_shared.py `sample_lines` uses diversity-first sampling; reuse it for any sample-based equivalence test.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: refined inline leaves are located by (model, capture name, regex)
- **Context**: STEREO, STEREOGROUP, ARROW_HEAD1/2, ARROW_BODY1, `[-#+~]` VISIBILITY are inline regex leaves, not variables.
- **Finding**: test_shared.REFINEMENTS pins each one by generated model id, capture name and exact regex. A regeneration (T07) that renames or moves them fails `test_refined_source_exists_in_generated_models` loudly.
- **Impact**: After regenerating models, update REFINEMENTS rather than the structured parts.
- **Confidence**: High
