## Observation: Lark makes lookahead-only guards optional
- **Context**: Diagnosing the Lark-accepts / jar-rejects disagreements in the 0.1.0 parity run.
- **Finding**: A model regex that is only a negative lookahead (`D_NOT_FORBIDDEN` in
  `model/diagrams/description.yaml:78`) is emitted by `tools/emit/lark.py` (nullable regex
  encoding) as `_o_224: /(?V0)(?=[\s\S])(?:(?!...))/ |` with an empty alternative, so the
  guard can be skipped. `foo` after a link in a description diagram parses in Lark
  (`sirimo-08-ceju218`, `tumepe-28-zuma441`). Not fixed (emitter change, out of T35 scope).
- **Impact**: Any guard written as a lookahead-only part has no effect in `dist/plantuml.lark`.
- **Confidence**: High (regex and rule text inspected; the encoding is documented in the lark.py docstring).

## Observation: 26 of 37 jar-rejected Lark accepts come from dropped entry keys
- **Context**: Interpreter run over the 37 blocks the jar and interpreter reject but Lark accepts.
- **Finding**: With every `reject`, `emit: false`, `require_pop`, `gives`/`needs` entry removed
  from the resolved models (`tools.emit.skip.skipped`), the interpreter accepts 26 of them.
  11 stay rejected; 3 of those are the lookahead case above, 8 are undiagnosed (vevatu-41,
  jokaxi-40, veducu-71 in both corpora; vega sub/subfile.puml; pdiff komoso-43).
- **Impact**: Documented in docs/semantic-gaps.md (generated by `tools/report/semantic_gaps.py`).
- **Confidence**: High for the 26 and the 3; the 8 are open.

## Observation: Lark timeouts at 10 s are Earley cost, not wrong verdicts
- **Context**: Re-ran the 62 timeout blocks (plantuml-ts 27, pdiff 35) with a 60 s budget, serially.
- **Finding**: 44 finish in 11.6-39.1 s and agree with jar and interpreter (all accepted); 18
  exceed 60 s (description 8, sequence 4, class 2, and 4 jar-rejected blocks vipixe-71 and
  kipive-74 in both corpora). The load average was 3-5 during the run, so this is not load noise.
- **Impact**: The earlier "load noise" explanation in the family notes holds only for the borderline
  cases (11-12 s); use `--timeout 60` to separate slow from wrong.
- **Confidence**: High.

## Observation: the pin lives in two places
- **Context**: Writing "Regenerating after an upstream bump".
- **Finding**: `upstream-pin.json` (extractor, oracle cache key) and `PIN_SHA` in `tools/config.py`
  (default jar name, provenance reader) must be changed together.
- **Impact**: Documented in the guide; a bump that changes only one gives a mixed pin.
- **Confidence**: High.
