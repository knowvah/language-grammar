## Observation: oracle counts PSystemUnsupported as accepted
- **Context**: Verifying L0 fixtures against the jar.
- **Finding**: `@startnope`, `@startchronology` and `@startjcckit` blocks come back `accepted: true`, `diagram_type: null`. `PSystemUnsupported` is not a `PSystemError` (Oracle.java only checks `instanceof PSystemError`). The sources are `PSystemErrorUtils.checkBasicError` :58-59 (UNKNOWN keyword) and `PSystemBuilder.java:283-284` (no factory has the type). The interpreter now mirrors this: `Dispatcher.plan` returns None, and the verdict is ok with `diagram`/`model` None.
- **Impact**: Parity treats unknown start keywords as accepted. Don't "fix" this in the interpreter without changing contract I5.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: oracle diagram_type is the diagram's type, not the factory's
- **Context**: Comparing `Verdict.diagram` with `diagram_type`.
- **Finding**: Oracle reports null for non-`TitledDiagram` results (license, version, math, latex, dot, ditaa, creole, def, the Welcome screen). `ListSpriteDiagram` is built as `DiagramType.HELP` (`klimt/sprite/ListSpriteDiagram.java:60`) although `ListSpriteDiagramFactory` is SPRITES. Model `diagram:` carries the factory type.
- **Impact**: Parity should compare `diagram` only when the oracle's is non-null, and must allow SPRITES vs HELP.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: gen-description-diagram accepts bare words the jar rejects
- **Context**: Single-line factory fixtures (`@startuml` + `licenses`, `emojis`, `arkban`).
- **Finding**: The interpreter accepts these via `gen-description-diagram`, but the jar rejects them. Upstream rejection happens outside the regexes, probably in execute/final checks. For accepted single-line inputs (`license`, `version`) the interpreter therefore attributes the block to DESCRIPTION, before the embedded single-line models are tried.
- **Impact**: The family task for description/usecase needs to close this. `tests/layers/test_document.py` restricts `start_map` for the single-line group for that reason.
- **Confidence**: High (oracle-verified)

## Observation: gen-psystem-ditaa holds only the SCALE helper pattern
- **Context**: `@startditaa` fixtures.
- **Finding**: `PSystemDitaaFactory` is a `PSystemBasicFactory` that accepts every non-empty body (`ditaa/PSystemDitaaFactory.java:58-121`). The extractor picked up its `SCALE` field (`scale=([\d.]+)`) as the only command. `model/embedded/embedded-ditaa.yaml` includes it and replaces it in dispatch.
- **Impact**: Translator/extractor (T06/T07) should treat PSystemDitaaFactory as opaque, or skip helper `Pattern` fields.
- **Confidence**: High

## Observation: a lone gantt separator is rejected by the jar
- **Context**: Choosing a `@startgantt` fixture body.
- **Finding**: `@startgantt` / `-- Phase one --` / `printscale weekly` is rejected by the jar but accepted by `gen-gantt-diagram`. `printscale weekly` alone is accepted by both.
- **Impact**: This is a gantt family gap.
- **Confidence**: Medium (cause not isolated)

## Observation: L0 block framing details
- **Context**: Mirroring `BlockUmlBuilder.java:100-135`.
- **Finding**:
  - `isStartDirective` is case-sensitive on "start" and needs at least one char after it, so `@STARTUML` and bare `@start` give no block. The keyword itself is case-insensitive and matched by prefix.
  - A nested `@start` discards the open block.
  - Any `@end…` closes a block, so `@startuml`…`@endmindmap` is fine.
  - An unterminated block is dropped.
  - Text outside blocks is ignored.
  - `PSystemWelcomeFactory` accepts only `@startuml` directly followed by its end line (comments allowed, blank lines not).
  - `removeInitialNoise` strips only leading noise.
- **Impact**: `tools/interp/source.split_blocks` and model/document.yaml encode these. The fixtures are in `tests/fixtures/document/forms`.
- **Confidence**: High
